# Privacy

Phrase Highlighter for Language Reactor does not collect personal data. There is no tracking, ads, analytics, or account.

- It only runs on languagereactor.com and www.languagereactor.com.
- It does not call extra servers. Meanings come from the dictionary Language Reactor already loaded in the page.
- Chrome is not given permission to reach other websites. The extension does not use `host_permissions`.
- The only thing saved on your computer is whether the sidebar is open or closed (`chrome.storage.local`). That stays on your device.
- There is no sign-in.

Uninstalling the extension removes it. Lesson text is never sent to the author.
